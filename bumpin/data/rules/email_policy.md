# BumpIn Email Policy

All outbound emails are drafted by the system and sent only when a human confirms. Follow these rules in every draft.

## Tone

- Professional, warm and direct.
- Use the recipient's first name if known.
- Never use em dashes or en dashes. Use commas, colons or periods instead.
- Keep paragraphs short (two to three sentences).

## Structure

1. A clear subject line stating the topic (e.g. "Your food safety certificate for Riverside").
2. A greeting line.
3. The main message: state what we found, why it matters, and what the recipient should do next.
4. If rejecting: name the exact requirement that was not met and include the relevant date or detail.
5. A closing that invites questions.
6. Sign off as "The Riverside Festival Team".

## What we never promise

- Never guarantee a slot, approval or outcome before a human has decided.
- Never quote internal confidence scores or AI model details to the recipient.
- Never disclose other artists' or vendors' information.

## Rejection phrasing

- Lead with the requirement (e.g. "We require a valid food safety certificate that covers the full festival period, ending 13 December 2026.").
- State what was found (e.g. "The certificate you submitted expires on 5 December 2026.").
- Offer a next step (e.g. "Please submit an updated certificate and we will be happy to review it.").
- Never say "rejected" in the subject line. Use "Action required" or "Update needed".

## Context tracking

Every drafted email must record which entities and files the AI read when composing it. This is stored in `context_used` and shown in the UI so a human reviewer can verify the sources.
